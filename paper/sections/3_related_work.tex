\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{Time-series foundation models.}
Moirai \citep{moirai}, Chronos \citep{chronos}, Time-MoE \citep{timemoe}, Timer \citep{timer},
TimesFM \citep{timesfm} and MOMENT \citep{moment} pretrain on large time-series corpora with
tokenisation, quantisation or patching designed for the modality, and are evaluated zero-shot on
held-out datasets. They set the accuracy we compare against, and they also set the resource
expectation we are trying to undercut: the corpora are measured in tens or hundreds of billions of
observations.

\paragraph{Forecasting with visual backbones.}
VisionTS \citep{visionts} renders a univariate series into a periodicity-aligned image, masks the
right-hand portion, and inpaints it with a frozen ImageNet masked autoencoder \citep{mae}; decoding
the completion gives a forecast that is competitive zero-shot without any time-series pretraining.
VisionTS++ \citep{visiontspp} keeps the image construction and continues pretraining the backbone
on LOTSA \citep{moirai}, with colourised multivariate rendering and a multi-quantile head, and
reports the strongest zero-shot numbers on this protocol.

Two points of contrast matter here. The visual prior that survives this transfer appears to be
small: SVTime \citep{svtime} matches VisionTS with a 215k-parameter model and OccamVTS
\citep{occamvts} distils a vision forecaster into $1\%$ of its parameters. And VisionTS++ does not
optimise pixel reconstruction alone --- it trains a multi-quantile (pinball) objective on the
forecast --- so the objective mismatch we report in Section~\ref{sec:diagnosis} is a statement about
the configuration we tested, VideoMAE's native masked-pixel objective continued on rendered series,
not a criticism of the image-based systems we compare with.

\paragraph{Video pretraining and future prediction.}
VideoMAE \citep{videomae} reconstructs masked spatiotemporal tubes, so its temporal structure is
implicit rather than a forward model; V-JEPA \citep{vjepa} and V-JEPA~2 \citep{vjepa2} predict
latent representations of masked regions and are used for planning, closer to the world-model
formulation of \citet{lecun2022} and \citet{dreamer}. We use both as initialisations. Our training
stage is world-model-\emph{style} in one sense only, that it maps an observed past to a predicted
future and is scored on that prediction; we claim nothing about action conditioning, planning or
physical reasoning, none of which we test.

\paragraph{Transfer regularisation.}
Continual pretraining of a strong backbone on a narrow corpus can erase the prior that motivated
choosing it. L2-SP \citep{l2sp} decays weights toward their initial values rather than toward zero.
It is an existing method, we use it only as an ablation arm, and we report that it helps at short
clip lengths and is inert in the configuration of our main model.
