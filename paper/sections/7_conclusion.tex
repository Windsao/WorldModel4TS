\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

A video-pretrained backbone is a useful starting point for forecasting but not a usable one:
continuing its own objective on rendered series improves that objective while degrading forecasts.
What makes the transfer work is a framework built around the forecasting task --- period-aligned
frames, prediction of a masked future block, a differentiable read-out that puts the loss in value
space, and a training scale distribution matched to the inference rule --- which reaches the lowest
mean MSE among the models we compare on the six-dataset zero-shot protocol after 60k updates on 654M
observations. Two results bound that claim: the video initialisation's lead closes by 20k updates,
so what we show is adaptation efficiency rather than a higher ceiling, and the transfer design is
what makes any initialisation usable at all.
